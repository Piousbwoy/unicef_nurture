Local-language audio guidance recordings.

Files are picked up automatically by lib/core/audio/audio_guide.dart using
the naming convention:

    <topic>_<language>.mp3

e.g.  child_danger_signs_dagbani.mp3
      newborn_danger_signs_hausa.mp3
      mother_danger_signs_gurene.mp3
      feeding_dagaare.mp3
      referral_english.mp3

Topics: child_danger_signs, newborn_danger_signs, mother_danger_signs,
feeding, referral.

Until recordings exist, the app shows and reads the plain-language scripts
instead — care never waits on an MP3.
