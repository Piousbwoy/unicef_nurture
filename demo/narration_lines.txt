Every year, mothers and children in the last mile die in the gap between a symptom and a health worker. CareBridge AI closes that gap, even with no network.
On a family's phone, Aura Nurture asks nothing of a mother except what she already knows. No forms, no medical words.
One tap per child. The app keeps a private record for the family, born on this device.
When something feels wrong, she starts a danger sign check. It is not a diagnosis. It is the same first questions a nurse would ask.
Every question is read aloud, in her own language, translated and spoken entirely on the phone. No data plan needed.
She answers yes to a danger sign. The app raises an alarm she can act on, and never ends the check. She stays in control.
The result tells her exactly what to do now, even on the way to the facility.
And she can ask her health worker for help, from the village, with no signal. The request holds the name and the urgency. Nothing else.
The moment any phone in the family reaches signal, the request travels through an offline outbox that never loses what a mother has already said.
At the CHPS compound, the community health worker sees the family on her phone. A queue that cannot silently drop.
Her record shows exactly what is known and what is missing, with confidence stated honestly.
The family sits at the top of her priorities, with one tap to assess or visit.
Recognition, decision, request, handoff. CareBridge AI. A chain that survives the last mile.
