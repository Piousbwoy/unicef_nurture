### CHPS
- Definition：Community-based Health Planning and Services — Ghana's primary community health delivery model. The app targets CHPS zones (3,000–4,500 population) and CHPS compounds as the operational unit for frontline health workers.
- Aliases：CHPS zone、CHPS compound

### CHO
- Definition：Community Health Officer — the frontline health worker who uses the app to conduct assessments, manage referrals, and plan daily visits. Primary user role with caseload-based scope.
- Aliases：community health officer

### FHW
- Definition：Frontline Health Worker — a broader role encompassing CHOs and other community-level health staff. The app supports FHW-specific tabs (Home, Visits, Families, Referrals, Profile).
- Aliases：frontline health worker

### IMCI
- Definition：Integrated Management of Childhood Illness — WHO clinical protocol used for child assessment (2–59 months) and young infant (0–59 days) classification. Defines exact thresholds for danger signs, pneumonia, diarrhea, malnutrition, and MUAC bands.
- Aliases：IMCI-Malnutrition、IMCI chart booklet

### MUAC
- Definition：Mid-Upper Arm Circumference — key malnutrition screening measurement. SAM <11.5cm, MAM 11.5–12.5cm, normal >12.5cm. Drives triage decisions and nutrition recommendations.
- Aliases：mid-upper arm circumference

### SAM
- Definition：Severe Acute Malnutrition — MUAC <11.5cm. Per IMCI/GHS protocol requires urgent inpatient referral (therapeutic feeding, F-75) or OTP enrollment with RUTF (Plumpy'Nut) depending on appetite and complications. Not treated with local foods alone.
- Aliases：severe acute malnutrition

### MAM
- Definition：Moderate Acute Malnutrition — MUAC 11.5–12.5cm. Treated with local-food counselling and complementary feeding guidance rather than therapeutic feeding.
- Aliases：moderate acute malnutrition

### ANC
- Definition：Antenatal Care — pregnancy assessment protocol for expectant mothers. Part of the maternal engines that flag pre-eclampsia (BP ≥140/90, severe ≥160/110) and other risk factors.
- Aliases：antenatal care

### PNC
- Definition：Postnatal Care — postpartum assessment protocol covering the period after delivery, including scenarios where a mother comes for check-up due to minor illness.
- Aliases：postnatal care

### EPI
- Definition：Expanded Programme on Immunisation — Ghana's national immunisation schedule (BCG, OPV0-3, IPV, Penta×3, PCV×3, Rota×2, MR×2, YF, MenA, malaria vaccine). The app includes catch-up planning for overdue vaccines.
- Aliases：Ghana EPI、immunisation schedule

### IYCF
- Definition：Infant and Young Child Feeding — nutritional guidance defining minimum dietary diversity (MDD ≥5/8 food groups) and minimum meal frequency (MMF 2× for 6–8mo, 3× for 9–23mo, 4× for non-breastfed).
- Aliases：infant and young child feeding

### RUTF
- Definition：Ready-to-Use Therapeutic Food — Plumpy'Nut, the standard treatment for SAM when appetite is intact. Referred via OTP (Outpatient Therapeutic Program) with weekly follow-up.
- Aliases：Plumpy'Nut、OTP

### MMDA
- Definition：Metropolitan, Municipal and District Assembly — administrative units in Northern Ghana. The app covers all 55 MMDAs across the 5 northern regions (Northern, North East, Savannah, Upper East, Upper West).
- Aliases：district、municipality

### TTFPP
- Definition：Technical Training Fellowship Programme — a Ghana Health Service training program. At least one team member must have TTFPP experience per hackathon eligibility requirements.
- Aliases：technical training fellowship programme

### CareBridge AI
- Definition：The project name — an offline-first, AI-assisted community health companion for frontline health workers and caregivers in Northern Ghana, built for the UNICEF Start-Up Lab 'AI for Nurturing Care' Hackathon 2026.
- Aliases：carebridge_ai

### Plan My Day
- Definition：A route optimization feature that combines GPS locations and household vulnerability scores to create a risk-ranked walking route for CHOs, helping them prioritize which families to visit first.
- Aliases：route optimizer、daily route planning

### Barrier Module
- Definition：A feature capturing why families did not seek care (no transport money, no NHIS card, husband's permission needed, facility closed, etc.) using icons and audio, then aggregating into a community barrier heatmap.
- Aliases：hidden barriers、barrier capture

### Sync Outbox
- Definition：An outbox pattern implementation where every record written locally is also queued for sync. Written in the same transaction as the record it describes, ensuring consistency between local data and sync state.
- Aliases：sync queue、outbox pattern

### Clinical Override
- Definition：Phase 10 of the workflow allowing health workers to override AI-generated recommendations. Ensures human remains responsible for care decisions while AI supports rather than replaces judgment.
- Aliases：human override、clinical override phase
